Every Axiom Cloud project has a plan, **Free** or **Pro**. The plan sets the
monthly allowances of the project's managed database environment. Allowances
reset at the start of each calendar month (UTC). The **Resources** page shows
the current figures; see [Acore backend databases on Free and Pro](/cloud/backend-databases)
for how measured usage appears there and what happens at 80% and at the limit.
Prices are published at general availability.

## Monthly allowances per project

| Allowance | Free | Pro with PostgreSQL | Pro with SQLite (libSQL) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Database storage | 100 MB | 1 GB | 100 MB |
| Rows read | 10,000,000 | 100,000,000 | 10,000,000 |
| Rows written | 25,000 | 1,000,000 | 25,000 |
| Database connections per server process | 2 | 10 | 2 |
| Requests | 10,000 | 100,000 | 100,000 |
| Execution time | 1,000 seconds | 10,000 seconds | 10,000 seconds |
| Egress | 100 MB | 1 GB | 1 GB |
| Authentication emails sent by Axiom | 50 | 1,000 | 1,000 |
| Resource operations (provision, retry, resume, restore and similar) | 20 | 100 | 100 |
| Hosted environments | 1 | 1 | 1 |

A Pro project that keeps SQLite keeps the Free database allowances (storage,
rows and connections) and gets the Pro request, execution, egress, email and
operation allowances. More database capacity on Pro needs PostgreSQL; see
[Upgrade a project](/database/project-upgrades).

Deleting a database, suspending it and installing or upgrading its
authentication tables never count as resource operations.

## Database counts

- A Free account can own at most **3** Free databases across all its projects
  (branches do not count).
- A Free account can run at most **60** resource operations a month across
  all its Free projects, so creating new projects does not reset the budget.
- A Pro database needs an active Pro subscription to be provisioned, retried
  or resumed.

## Authentication reserve

Managed application authentication has its own monthly reserve per
environment, so sign-ins never use your application's rows-written allowance.

| Entitlement | Free | Pro with SQLite (libSQL) | Pro with PostgreSQL |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Authentication rows written (reserve) | 25,000 | 25,000 | 250,000 |
| Rows per user (sign-ins and token refreshes) | 6,000 | 20,000 | 60,000 |
| New users per day | 100 | 100 | 1,000 |

When a user reaches the per-user figure, that user's sign-ins and refreshes are
refused with `AXAUTH_QUOTA` for the rest of the month. When the reserve is
used up, new users are refused first; existing users keep signing in.
Signing out, revoking sessions, banning users and changing passwords or roles
are never refused for quota.

## Preview branches

| Limit | Free | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Default lifetime | 24 hours | 72 hours |
| Longest lifetime | 72 hours | 7 days |
| Live branches per project | 1 | 3 |
| Branch creations per day | 3 | 10 |
| Branch storage | 100 MB | 100 MB |

Preview branches are available to selected projects during the Alpha. See
[Preview branches](/cloud/branches).

## Restore and backups

| Limit | SQLite (libSQL) | PostgreSQL |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Point-in-time restore window | Last 24 hours | Last 6 hours |
| Daily backups kept | 7 days | 7 days |
| Previous copy kept after a restore or move | 24 hours | 24 hours |

Daily backups and Owner restores are turned on for selected projects only
today, and will be on for every project at general availability. At most one
previous copy is kept at a time. Every retention period is published with the
operations policy at general availability.

## Platform-wide limits

Some limits protect the shared service rather than one project. They can
refuse a request even when your project is under its own allowances:

- Shared SQLite (libSQL) capacity near its provider limit can stop the largest
  consumers until shared usage falls.
- Axiom's own authentication mail has a daily platform budget. Projects that
  send through their own SMTP server are not limited by it.
- Cloud sign-in codes have a cooldown of 60 seconds per address.
