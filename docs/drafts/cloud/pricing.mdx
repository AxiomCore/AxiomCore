Axiom Cloud is priced per project. A subscription funds the included
resources of one project; upgrading one project never upgrades the other
projects in the workspace.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | What it includes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | No charge | One hosted environment with a managed SQLite (libSQL) database and the [Free allowances](/cloud/limits#monthly-allowances-per-project) |
| Pro | $20 USD per project per month (launch price) | One hosted environment with managed PostgreSQL, or SQLite if you keep it, and the [Pro allowances](/cloud/limits#monthly-allowances-per-project) |

Taxes, overage pricing and annual billing are not published yet (TODO: add
them when the paid release defines them). No allowance on this page is sold
as an overage today: at a limit, the service refuses or narrows the work as
described in [Limits](/cloud/limits).

The following need separately funded allowances and are not part of either
plan: additional environments, SMS delivery, AI inference and dedicated
database capacity. Phone verification, when offered, uses your own Twilio
account and Twilio bills you directly.

## How a subscription works

- Selecting Pro saves a preference. Payment details are collected by
  Stripe-hosted Checkout.
- A browser success redirect alone never activates paid access. The verified
  subscription state must synchronize first.
- A Pro preference alone does not authorize hosting. Provisioning and resuming
  a Pro database need an active Pro subscription.

## When a payment fails or a subscription ends

A failed payment or a cancelled subscription never deletes data.

| Subscription state | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Active | Normal service |
| Past due, unpaid, incomplete or paused | New database provisioning, retries and resumes are refused. Runtime database credentials for your servers are refused with a payment code. Authentication for your users keeps working. Nothing is deleted. |
| Cancelled | As past due. The data stays in place. |

Deletion of a hosted database only happens when a project Owner asks for it.
A Free project that stays inactive may be archived in the future; an archive
keeps the data and resumes on use. Automatic inactivity archiving is not
turned on for customers yet.
